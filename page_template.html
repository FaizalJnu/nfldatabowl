The Sticks Clock

3rd and 4th down against man coverage: when does a first-down throw come open, how long does it stay open, and when does the ball come out?

A window is a wide receiver or tight end with 2+ yards on the nearest coverage defender, at or past the first-down line, for at least 0.3 seconds. 2021 season, Weeks 1–8, league-wide.

The number is pulled toward the average for his position because samples are small; the line is the 90% range and the dashed mark is his position's average. Receivers who may win later in the play look worse here. This is not a Sticks Clock number: the clock is league-wide only.

Read this before you use it

  • Tracking stops at the throw. A window that would have opened later is never seen, so opening times lean early.
  • Time open is a survival estimate, because the throw cuts off windows that are still open. Quarterbacks tend to throw to open receivers, so the throw is not independent of the window.
  • Open means distance to the nearest coverage defender. It does not account for pass rush, throwing lanes or the quarterback's read.
  • Sacks, scrambles and plays with no tagged release are left out.
